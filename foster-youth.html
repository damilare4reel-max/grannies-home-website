THP-NMD Non-Profit Program

A Steady Start On The Road To Independence

Grannies Home Foundation provides stable transitional housing and real support for young people in Calexico ages 18–21, aging out of extended foster care.

A real home base, real skills, and real people in your corner as you build an independent life.

Our Mission

Every year, young people age out of foster care with nowhere to go.

Grannies Home Foundation exists to change that outcome, one person at a time — with real housing, real guidance, and people who stay.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Who is eligible for this program?

Young people ages 18–21 who are currently in California's Extended Foster Care program and referred through the county's Independent Living unit.

How long can someone stay?

Housing is designed to support residents through their time in Extended Foster Care, with the goal of building real independence before transitioning out.

Is there a cost to residents?

The program is designed to operate through county rate funding for THP-NMD placements, not out-of-pocket cost to the young person.

What support is actually included?

Stable housing plus life-skills coaching covering budgeting, job readiness, and daily independent-living skills, along with ongoing check-ins.

How do I refer someone or apply?

Referrals typically go through Imperial County Child Welfare Services. Reach out using the contact page and we'll walk you through the next steps.
