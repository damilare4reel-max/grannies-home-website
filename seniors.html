Common Questions

Frequently Asked

Is Grannies Home a licensed care facility?

No. Grannies Home operates as independent living for seniors 62 and older. We provide housing, meals, and housekeeping, not personal care or medical supervision.

Who is eligible to live here?

Residents must be 62 or older and able to live independently, including bathing, dressing, mobility, and managing their own medications.

What's included in rent?

A private furnished room, home-cooked meals, regular housekeeping, and basic utilities. Personal care services are not included, since we operate as independent living only.

What if my needs change over time?

We stay in touch with residents and families. If needs change, we help identify next steps, whether that's outside home care or a licensed care setting.

Can I schedule a visit before deciding?

Yes, we encourage it. Reach out using the form below or call us directly to set up a time to see the home in person.
