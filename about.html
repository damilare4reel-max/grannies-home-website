About Grannies Home

Two homes. One mission of care.

Creating stable, welcoming places where people can live independently, build confidence, and feel like they belong.

Our Philosophy

Real homes. Real people. Real support.

Grannies Home is built around a simple belief: housing should feel human. For seniors, that means an independent home where privacy and routine are respected. For young adults transitioning out of foster care, it means a stable place to land while learning how to build the next chapter.
